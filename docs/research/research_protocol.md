# Research Protocol

For every candidate paper, dataset, repository, API, or technical source record:

1. Title and year
2. Author/organization
3. Dataset/source
4. Available features/signals
5. Sampling rate/resolution
6. Format and access method
7. License/access restrictions
8. Method/algorithm
9. Main results
10. Evaluation methodology
11. Limitations
12. Reproducibility
13. Possible project use
14. Possible research gap/improvement

## Decision Questions

- Do we actually have the required data?
- Is provenance clear?
- Is academic use legally permitted?
- Is the resolution sufficient?
- Can results be validated?
- What existing work are we extending?

No novelty claim is accepted without evidence.
